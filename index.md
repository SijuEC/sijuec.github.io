## Weight Management Tool

At Recaro Aircraft Seating, I made a Weight Management Tool to analyze and manage weight data. This user-friendly tool cuts the time needed for the job by 70%, making life easier for our analysts. It's hosted on our internal server and helps analysts in Germany, the USA, Poland, and China work more efficiently.

The tool uses FastAPI for the backend, PostgreSQL for the database, and Streamlit for the frontend. I use GitLab for version control and Docker to package everything together. The best part? It saves us over €42,000 a year. Efficiency has never been so simple!
